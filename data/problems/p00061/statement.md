# Team Query

- CodeNet ID: `p00061`
- Dataset: `AIZU`
- Time limit: `1000 ms`
- Memory limit: `131072 KB`

Each record gives a team number and its score, separated by a comma. The list
ends with `0,0`; the remaining lines are team numbers to query.

## Input

```text
1,20
2,10
0,0
1
2
```

## Output

```text
20
10
```
